Personal OS · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026.

Personal OS is a private planning app. Each invited person has their own separate copy (an “instance”) with its own database. This policy explains what that instance holds, where it lives, and what it does with information from Google Calendar if you connect it.

Where your information lives

  • Your instance runs on one privately owned computer. It is reachable only over a private network to which you are personally invited; it is not on the public internet.
  • Your records are kept in your instance’s own database, separate from every other person’s. Encrypted backups are kept on the same computer.
  • The person who runs that computer, who invited you, has administrator access to it and could in principle read what is stored there. If that matters to you, talk to them before you start.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Personal OS asks Google for three permissions:

  • See the list of your calendars, so you can choose which ones to use.
  • View events on your calendars, read-only, so your plans can work around them. Only the calendars you choose are read, from the start of today through the next thirty days.
  • Create a calendar of its own and manage its events, so your plans and protected time appear in a calendar called “Personal OS”. It cannot create, change or delete events on any other calendar.

Events read from your calendars are stored in your instance’s database, where they appear as fixed commitments in your planner. The access Google grants is stored on the same computer, readable only by your instance, and is never shown or sent anywhere else.

Personal OS does not sell your information, use it for advertising, share it with other people or companies, or use it to train AI models.

Personal OS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your own AI assistant

If you choose to connect your own AI assistant (for example Claude Desktop) to your instance, it can read your Personal OS records, including events imported from Google Calendar, and make changes you approve, but only when you ask it to, using your own assistant account. Nothing is sent to an assistant you have not connected yourself.

Stopping and deleting

  • Disconnect Google Calendar on the Google Calendar page in your instance’s Settings. This withdraws the access at Google and deletes the stored grant. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The “Personal OS” calendar stays in your Google account after you disconnect, for you to keep or delete.
  • You can download everything in your instance at any time from its export page, and ask the person who invited you to delete your instance and its backups entirely.

Questions

Personal OS is shared privately by invitation. For any question about your information, or to ask for it to be deleted, contact the person who invited you.
