Personal OS

A private planner, shared with a few friends.

Personal OS helps one person plan their week: it keeps their tasks, goals, commitments and logs, and fits the work into the free time around what is already on their calendar.

It is not a public service. Each invited person gets their own separate copy, running on one privately owned computer and reachable only over a private network. There is no sign-up, no advertising and no subscription.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Personal OS reads the calendars you pick so your plans can work around your existing events, and it writes your plans into one calendar of its own, called “Personal OS”. It cannot change any other calendar. You can disconnect at any time.

How your information is handled is set out in the privacy policy.

Personal OS is shared privately by invitation. If you were invited and have a question, ask the person who invited you.
